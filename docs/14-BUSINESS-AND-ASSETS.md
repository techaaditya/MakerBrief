# Business discovery and asset register

Start with one willing maker and a small catalog. Ask what they currently use to settle details, what causes revisions or disputes, and whether a saved brief would replace work. Do not pitch automatic return reduction or guaranteed conversion.

Possible price tests: a bounded monthly workspace or per-approved-brief package. Treat any proposed price as a question for customers. Compute contribution after actual provider units, storage, hosting, payment fees and support. Free credits do not establish sustainable unit economics.

The included jacket.svg is an original vector sample, not an image of a real garment or person. Catalog options and prices are fictional. Every real source photo, maker reference and public output must have appropriate permission. Attribution is not a substitute for permission.

For cultural attire, record maker-supplied names and descriptions, contributor approval, materials and origin evidence. Do not let a language model invent community meanings or flatten distinct traditions into one generic style. The archive supplies no cultural provenance dataset.

A real pilot should document a customer's task, the maker's review, unresolved details and whether they would pay. Keep personal data out of the public evaluation report.
